Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LaTeX Editor ("we", "our", or "the application"), accessible at https://latex-ide.ronitmehta817.workers.dev/, handles user data.

1. Overview & Architecture

LaTeX Editor is a client-side web application. All LaTeX compilation, rendering, and document processing happen entirely in your web browser using WebAssembly. We do not operate external database servers that collect or store your documents or personal data.

2. Google User Data & Drive Integration

If you choose to sign in with your Google account, the application requests access to the following scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

  • Application Data Folder: This permission only grants access to a hidden, application-specific folder within your Google Drive. LaTeX Editor cannot see, access, modify, or delete any of your other Google Drive files.
  • Purpose: We access this folder solely to save and synchronize your LaTeX documents and user settings across your own devices.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your Google tokens, profile information, and document contents are never transferred to, sold to, or shared with third parties or external servers.

3. Local Storage

The application uses your browser's local storage and session storage to temporarily save the currently active document and keep you logged in during your browsing session. You can clear this data at any time via your browser settings.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

LaTeX Editor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.
